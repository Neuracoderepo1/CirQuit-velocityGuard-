The failure mode

AI agents can create catastrophic spend before conventional observability or billing systems have time to react.

A stuck retry loop, a runaway agent, or a leaked key can spend a month's budget in an hour. Billing dashboards tell you what happened after it's already expensive. CirQuit decides before every call executes.
